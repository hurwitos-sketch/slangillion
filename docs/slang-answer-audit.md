## Matching and retry rules

1. Normalize Unicode, case, apostrophes, punctuation, and whitespace. Preserve word boundaries, supported laughter emoji, and negation.
2. Match canonical forms before explicit, question-specific aliases. All aliases inherit their canonical score.
3. Apply reviewed laughter elongations and final-letter stretches only when a unique known expression results.
4. Permit one unambiguous token correction: adjacent transposition, doubled key, a missing interior character in a long word, or one interior substitution in a long word with intact boundaries. Never remove words or make broad short-word substitutions. Multiple possible canonical answers reject.
5. Reject unknown input without writing progress, adding a result, changing the index or score, resetting the deadline, or triggering a reveal. Keep the editable input and show “Slang not recognized! Try another word.” Editing clears this error.
6. The original 25-second deadline continues during retries. Expiration still scores zero once. A recognized expression locks once and uses the existing reveal and Next Round flow.

## Scoring review

The original tier names, colors, icons, point values, and animations are retained. Their names are visual branding; current scores are provisional editorial judgments of expressiveness, cultural relevance, specificity, and quality, not measured player frequencies. No region or community is treated as inherently less valid. Ordinary synonyms remain at the low end. Older or obscure phrases do not automatically receive 100 points. Examples: crash out, financially cooked, and spill the tea can outscore longer familiar idioms. Every registered alias has exactly its canonical answer's score.

Legacy saved rounds are validated against the previous frozen bank and retain their original scores. New recognized rounds record recognition version 2. Existing completed games are not restarted or silently rescored.

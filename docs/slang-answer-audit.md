## Requested changes

- Friend includes dawg, bro/brah/bruh/bruv, fam, ace, homeslice, my guy/guy, fella, and jit. Close spelling forms share scores. This category includes familiar forms of address, not only literal close friends. Jit is regional address for a younger person/acquaintance, not a universal friendship synonym.
- Money includes racks, scratch, millis, bands, bag, and motion. Motion is specifically the requested financial activity/income/hustle sense, not a literal unit of currency. Cash is removed.
- Attractive excludes hot/hottie/hot af, cute/cutie, sexy, gorgeous, stunning, pretty, beautiful, handsome, and good looking. It accepts fine shit/fine shyt, baddie, dime, talent, eye candy, and dollface. Talent uses the British informal attractive-people sense. Fine shit is explicit/vulgar language, included by request.
- Generic dictionary synonyms such as annoyed, irritated, talk/talking, awesome, brag, and boast are also removed from their current categories. Slang, figurative idioms, and explicitly requested forms of address remain.
- Distinct expressions are not merged merely because they share a meaning. Bro/brah are aliases, while dawg, fam, ace, and homeslice remain separate entries.

## Scoring and recognition

The four live levels are Street Level (20), Subway (50), Trenches (85), and The Underground (100). Caves is removed. Exactly one canonical expression per category earns The Underground, including that expression’s accepted aliases. Scores are editorial estimates, never measured frequencies or random assignments. Regional origin alone does not make an answer high-tier. Each category retains accepted answers at every tier; the maximum daily score remains 700.

Recognition is question-specific: normalized canonical match, explicit alias, safe repeated-letter variation, then conservative unambiguous typo matching. Negation and word boundaries are preserved. Removed plain synonyms do not become accepted merely through their old aliases.

Unknown input remains editable on the same question with no score, result, progress, or deadline change. The original 25-second timer continues. A valid answer locks once.

Recognition version 1/unmarked rounds retain their original bank. Version 2 rounds are checked against frozen pre-rebalance data and matching code. Version 3 rounds use a frozen pre-four-tier bank and matcher. New rounds record recognition version 4. Previously earned scores and completed games are not erased when current answers are removed or re-tiered.
